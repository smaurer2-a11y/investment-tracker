Why individual stocks, not ETFs

Dual US/Austrian citizen — both regimes penalize pooled funds, so the equity sleeve is single-name stocks:

  • UCITS (EU-domiciled) ETFs trigger US PFIC reporting/tax — the iShares S&P 500 fund is the live example, hence the sale.
  • US-domiciled ETFs trigger Austrian Nichtmeldefonds pauschal taxation.
  • Individual stocks stay clean on both sides.
